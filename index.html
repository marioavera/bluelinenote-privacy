BlueLineNote LLC

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy covers the three apps published by BlueLineNote LLC: BlueLineNote, Investigator Suite and Claim Notes. Where a section applies to only some of them, it says so.

Your work stays on your device

All reports, notes, case folders, claims, scans, photos, and other content you enter into any of the apps remain on your device and are never transmitted to the developer or any external server. The developer does not have access to your notes, reports, case folders, claims, scans, or any content you create. None of the apps provides cloud storage or a remote database for your content, and none of them queries any external database about any person.

Investigator SuiteClaim Notes Scanned identity documents

These apps can read a driver's licence or identification card using your device's camera — either the printed text on the card or, where the card has one, the barcode on its back. The reading happens entirely on your device using Apple's and Google's on-device frameworks. The image and the information read from it are never uploaded, and no recognition happens on any server.

What is read — a name, date of birth, sex, identification number, address, or phone number — is always shown to you for review and correction before it is added to a case folder or a claim, and it is stored only on your device. In Claim Notes, an ID you scan and save for later is also kept only on your device until you attach it to a claim or delete it.

Investigator Suite Information about other people

Investigator Suite is a documentation tool for professional investigators. You enter and store information about people connected to your own casework — names, photographs, and notes you record yourself.

The app does not look anyone up. It performs no search against any public record, database, watchlist, or third-party service, and it does not perform facial recognition or biometric identification of any person. Everything it shows you is something you put there. Any connections it displays between cases are drawn only from names you have entered yourself, on your own device.

You are responsible for having a lawful basis to record and retain information about other people, and for following the rules of your agency, licence, or jurisdiction.

Claim Notes Information about other people

Claim Notes is a documentation tool for insurance and claims professionals. You enter and store information about people connected to your own claims — drivers, vehicle owners, occupants, injured parties, and property owners — along with photographs and notes you record yourself.

The app does not look anyone up. It performs no search against any public record, database, or third-party service, and it does not perform facial recognition or biometric identification of any person. Everything it shows you is something you entered or scanned yourself, on your own device.

You are responsible for having a lawful basis to record and retain information about other people, and for following the rules of your employer, carrier, licence, or jurisdiction.

Backup & Restore

You can create a password-protected backup of your saved records, case folders, claims, and images. The backup is a single encrypted file, locked with a password that only you know — the developer cannot open it, and cannot recover or reset a lost password. You choose where to save it (your own Files, your own cloud storage, or email) using your device's share sheet or save dialog, and you restore by selecting that file. Backup files are never sent to or stored by the developer.

Investigator SuiteClaim Notes App lock

You can require Face ID, Touch ID, a fingerprint, or your device passcode to open the app. Authentication is performed entirely by your device's operating system. The app is told only whether the check succeeded — it never receives, stores, or transmits your face, fingerprint, or passcode.

Licensing and feature limits

To validate purchases, activate agency seats, and enforce free-tier usage limits, the apps send a device identifier — and, for metered features, a count of how many times those features have been used — to the developer's server (api.bluelinenote.app).

This identifier is deliberately durable: on Android it is derived from a system value and on iOS it is stored in the device keychain, so it survives deleting and reinstalling the app. That is what keeps a free-use allowance from resetting on reinstall. It is a random or system-assigned value only. It is not your name, email, account, phone number, or advertising identifier, and it cannot be used to identify you.

This information is used only to operate the free, paid, and agency licensing. It contains no personal information and none of your content, and it is never sold or shared with advertisers or other third parties for advertising or tracking.

Anonymous usage analytics

The apps collect a small amount of optional anonymous usage data — for example, which screens are opened, how long each screen is used, time in the app, the language the app is set to, device model, operating system version, and app version. In BlueLineNote this also includes your subscription type.

In Claim Notes, usage data is limited to counts of how often features are used (for example, claims created or PDFs exported), the app language, whether app lock and dark mode are turned on, the platform, and a random identifier created by the app. It is sent at most once a day to a spreadsheet the developer keeps with Google, which stores it on the developer's behalf.

This data is not linked to your identity, contains no personal information and none of your content — no names, case or claim numbers, policy numbers, addresses, photographs, amounts, or locations — and is identified only by a random value generated by the app, which resets if the app is reinstalled. It is not used for tracking across other apps or websites, and it is not sold or shared with advertisers.

You can turn it off at any time: in Investigator Suite under Settings → Usage analytics, in BlueLineNote under Backup & Restore → Anonymous Usage, and in Claim Notes under Settings → Share anonymous usage data. Turning it off changes nothing about how the app works; in Claim Notes it also deletes any counts not yet sent.

Feedback you choose to send

If you send feedback from within an app, the message you write and basic technical details about the app and device are sent to the developer so it can be read and answered. Only send what you are comfortable sharing, and do not include information from your cases or claims.

Legal requests

We may disclose the limited information we hold if required by law or in response to a valid legal request, and we cooperate with lawful requests from authorities. That information is limited to a randomly generated or system-assigned device identifier, licensing or usage records, and any feedback you chose to send — it is not your name, account, or any personal identifier. Your reports, notes, case folders, claims, and scans remain on your device, are never transmitted to us, and are not accessible to us — we cannot produce that content.

Data retention

We keep the limited licensing and anonymous usage data described above only as long as needed to operate the free, paid, and agency licensing and to keep the service working. Because this data is tied only to a random or system-assigned identifier and contains no personal information, it is not linked to you.

Content you create lives only on your device. Deleting the app deletes that content, and deleting an individual case folder, claim, note, or image removes it from the device. Copies you have made yourself — backup files or exported PDFs — stay wherever you saved them until you delete them.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the limited data we collect in transit to and on our server. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but because your reports, notes, case folders, claims, and scans never leave your device, the most sensitive information you create is never exposed to us or to anyone in transit.

Children

None of the apps is directed to children, and none is intended for use by anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Platform services

The apps use platform services such as in-app purchases, speech recognition, on-device text and barcode recognition, the camera and document scanner, biometric authentication, location, and the photo library. Depending on your device, these are handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. Each is used only for the purpose described in the permission prompt shown when you first use the feature.

Your responsibility

You are responsible for securing your device and for how you store, manage, and use any information you enter into the apps. You are also responsible for following your department's, agency's, employer's, or company's policies and procedures, your professional licensing requirements, and applicable laws governing the documentation, handling, retention, and sharing of any information you record.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new date above. Continuing to use any of the apps after a change means you accept the revised policy.
